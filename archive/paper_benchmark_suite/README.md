# Archived Paper Benchmark Suite

This directory preserves the older RQ1-RQ5 paper benchmark framework and its
generated outputs.

It was moved out of `experiments/` so the active experiment area can stay
focused on the two current research tracks:

- `experiments/elephant_context/`
- `experiments/long_term_memory_graph/`

Treat the contents here as historical archive material unless you explicitly
decide to revive that benchmark suite.
