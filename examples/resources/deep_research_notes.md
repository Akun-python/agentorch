# Deep Research Notes

## Core Principles
- Separate evidence collection, reasoning, and final synthesis.
- Prefer grounded evidence over unsupported intuition.
- If local knowledge is insufficient, escalate to web search when available.
- Keep citations, uncertainty, and coverage gaps visible.

## Architecture Patterns
- Supervisor-worker.
- Planner-executor.
- Critic loop.
- Blackboard/shared memory.
